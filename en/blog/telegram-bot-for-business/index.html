Telegram bots for business: 6 practical uses

Where a bot replaces a website or an operator, and when it genuinely helps sales.

In Armenia and the CIS people open Telegram every day, so a bot is often the shortest path from a customer to an order. There is nothing to install and no sign-up: the customer just taps “Start”. Here are six situations where a bot really works.

1. Pre-orders

Cafés, bakeries, restaurants: customers order before they arrive and you see the order in advance. Shorter queues, more orders.

2. Online booking

Salons, clinics, gyms: the bot shows free slots, books the visit and sends a reminder. Staff stop spending hours in chat threads.

3. Delivery without middlemen

Your own bot with a menu, payments and delivery status. You keep the full order value and the customer base stays yours.

4. Leads from your website

Every request from your site lands in your Telegram instantly, so you reply within minutes, not the next day. That is exactly how the brief on the BERD SOFT site works.

5. Internal processes

Staff reports, stock levels, alerts about new orders. A bot can become a handy dashboard for your team.

6. Customer support

Answers to common questions at any hour, with complex questions handed over to a person.

Where to start

Pick the one process that eats the most time today and start there. A good bot is simple: three or four buttons and a clear outcome. If you want to discuss your idea, fill in the brief on our site.
