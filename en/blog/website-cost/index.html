How much does a website cost? What drives the price

Why two websites can differ in price tenfold, and how to understand what you are paying for.

The honest answer to “how much does a website cost” is always the same: it depends on what the site has to do. Two sites can look alike, yet one simply describes a company while the other takes orders and payments and sends data to a CRM. These are the factors that actually set the price.

1. The type of site

A landing page is one page with one goal, such as collecting leads from ads. A website has several pages: services, work, contact. An online store adds a catalog, cart and checkout. A web app (CRM, client portal, booking) is already software with real business logic.

2. Features

Every feature is work in the design and on the server:

  • online payments and invoices,
  • online booking with schedules and reminders,
  • an admin panel so you can edit content yourself,
  • integration with a CRM, 1C or Telegram,
  • several languages.

3. Design: template or custom

A ready-made template is cheaper, but your site will look like hundreds of others. A custom design is made for your brand and your customers. When a site has to sell, the difference shows in the results.

4. What happens after launch

A site needs hosting, a domain, SSL, backups and updates. Ask your contractor who is responsible for these and whose name the domain and server are registered in. At BERD SOFT they are always registered to the client.
