Instagram is not a website: why a business needs both

Instagram brings attention, a website brings trust and orders. How they work together.

Many businesses in Armenia start on Instagram, and that is the right call. But at some point customers get lost in DMs and new ones cannot find you on Google. Here is why Instagram and a website complement each other rather than compete.

Instagram is for attention

It is great for showing, inspiring and staying top of mind. But an algorithm decides who sees your post, and an account can be blocked at any time. It is rented space.

Your website is yours

A website works 24/7 and does not depend on algorithms. Customers find prices and terms there and order without waiting for a DM reply.

Google searches websites

When someone types “dentist Yerevan” or “website development”, Google shows websites, not Instagram posts. Without a site you are missing exactly where people already look for you.

Trust

Partners and larger clients check your website before signing. A good site shows that the business is serious and here to stay.

How to connect the two

  • put your website link in your Instagram bio,
  • show your work on the site and add a request form,
  • send requests to Telegram so you reply fast,
  • measure how many people come from Instagram and what they do on the site.

That way Instagram brings people in and your website turns them into customers.
